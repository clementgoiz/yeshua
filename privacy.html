Yeshua Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Yeshua Uploader is a private application used solely by the owner of the YouTube channels “Yeshua Aramaic” and “Yeshua Animation” to publish his own videos. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the user agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Data accessed

With the owner’s authorization (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl), the application only: uploads videos and Shorts to the authorized channel, adds their subtitles, and reads the authorized channel’s identifier and title to check that it is the right channel. It does not read, collect or process any data about other users, viewers, comments or subscribers.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner’s own computer and are used only to publish to his channels. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account security page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens unusable; they are then deleted.
