Yeshua – Daily Mass Readings

Yeshua publishes the readings of the Catholic Mass of the day as videos, every day, on two YouTube channels:

  • Yeshua Aramaic – the readings read in Aramaic, the language of Jesus (Syriac Peshitta text, reconstructed 1st-century Galilean pronunciation), with Syriac, transliteration and French subtitles.
  • Yeshua Animation – the same readings in animation, in English, for all audiences.

Voices, images and animation are generated with AI. Biblical texts are never generated: they come from the Peshitta (ETCBC), the World English Bible (public domain) and AELF.

Yeshua Uploader

“Yeshua Uploader” is a private tool used only by the owner of these two channels to upload each day’s videos, Shorts and subtitles to his own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users.
